# Bookshelf artwork sources

All ten spines are cropped from real scans or photographs available on the internet. No spine lettering, logos, or texture was generated or redrawn. Artwork remains the property of its respective publishers, designers, photographers, and booksellers.

Some editions differ from the previous illustrated shelf. Corresponding front-cover scans or photos were changed at the same time to keep each visible spine and front cover consistent. Source wear, cloth texture, and small photographic imperfections are intentional.

## Jacket artwork and photographs

These photographs were cropped and flattened using their four measured corners. Only perspective correction, cropping, and WebP encoding were applied. The lettering remains the original photographed print. The thinner Sun and Steel and Industrial Society editions retain narrower shelf widths.

- **Steve Jobs:** Simon & Schuster jacket artwork reproduced in [iClarified's 2011 article](https://www.iclarified.com/16502/front-and-back-cover-of-the-upcoming-steve-jobs-biography-photo). [Exact jacket image](https://www.iclarified.com/images/news/16502/57164/57164-500.png). Spine cropped at x=227, y=8, width=46, height=321. The matching front cover now uses the publisher’s [1399 × 2131 jacket](https://d28hgpri8am2if.cloudfront.net/book_images/onix/cvr9781451648539/steve-jobs-9781451648539_hr.jpg).
- **Meditations:** Classics Club / Walter J. Black cloth edition. [Spine listing](https://www.ebay.com/itm/167267589698), [exact spine photo](https://i.ebayimg.com/images/g/cJUAAOSw029nkJIu/s-l1600.jpg). [Matching front-cover listing](https://www.ebay.com/itm/226548908960), [exact front photo](https://i.ebayimg.com/00/s/MTIwMFgxNjAw/z/uA0AAOSwrjlmwTix/$_57.JPG?set_id=880000500F). This replaces the Modern Library bird-cover edition because a verified matching spine scan could not be located for that edition.
- **Designing Data-Intensive Applications:** O'Reilly first edition, ISBN 9781449373320. [Bookbot listing](https://bookbot.com/g/518366), [exact front and spine photograph](https://rezised-images.knhbt.cz/1920x1920/93538553.webp). Both panels are extracted from this photograph, replacing the previous illustrative cover.
- **Sun and Steel:** Kodansha International 1990 paperback printing, ISBN 9780870111174. [Second Story Books listing](https://www.secondstorybooks.com/pages/books/1384666/yukio-mishima-john-bester/sun-and-steel?soldItem=true), [exact front and spine photo](https://secondstorybooks.cdn.bibliopolis.com/pictures/1384666.jpg?width=1600). This is a very thin volume, so the source's spine is narrower and softer than the flat scans.
- **Industrial Society and Its Future:** Graphyco edition. [Knihobot listing](https://knihobot.cz/g/5483465), [exact front and spine photograph](https://rezised-images.knhbt.cz/1920x1920/81330837.webp). Both panels are extracted from this photograph, replacing the previous illustrative cover.
