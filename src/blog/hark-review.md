**Rating: 10/10**

**Not an ad:** nobody paid me, sponsored this, or asked me to write it. Full disclosure: I signed up early as an interested user, and Hark gave me their $100-a-month plan free for a month. That's how I got to push it this hard. Everything below is my honest take.

I've used every AI chatbot you can name. You type a question, you get a nicely formatted paragraph, you copy it somewhere, and then you do the actual work yourself. Hark is the first one that broke that loop for me, and after a month I'm convinced it's what the next generation of AI looks like.

The short version: it doesn't just answer. It *does*.

## It actually uses a computer

Hark runs real browsers on its own machines. It signs in to sites, fills in forms, clicks through dashboards, and comes back with the result. Things I used to put off for weeks because they meant forty tabs and three logins just quietly get done while I'm at work. When something needs me (a code, an approval, a yes or no), it asks, and only then.

## It remembers me

There's no "new chat" button, and that turns out to be the whole point. It's one long conversation with something that keeps track of my preferences, my projects, my routines and the things I said I'd do. I never re-explain context. It knows what I'm working toward and quietly gets the details right.

## It lives in my accounts, carefully

With my mail, calendar, code and notes connected, it can triage an inbox, reshape a week, review a pull request, or file research straight into my notes. And it asks before anything that matters: spending money, sending something in my name, or anything public. I've never had it go rogue on me.

## It works in parallel

This is the absurd part. Ask it for a big piece of research and it spins up several browsers at once, each chasing a different angle, then hands back one clean comparison. Deep market research, legal precedent digs, competitor teardowns, product audits: work that would eat my weekend comes back in the time it takes to make dinner.

## It builds things

It writes and ships code, builds web pages, makes documents, slides and study guides, and has even put live panels on my home screen that keep themselves updated. I've had it test software by playing through thousands of rounds and report back what broke.

## It's proactive without being annoying

Morning rundowns, reminders that land at the right time, watches on things I care about, and the occasional nudge about something I forgot. It feels less like an app and more like a sharp friend who has my back.

## The ADHD angle

I have ADHD, and the hardest part of my day is never the work itself. It's starting, switching and remembering. Hark takes the switching and remembering off my plate. That alone has changed how much I get done.

## Anything I'd change?

Honestly, not much. It's early, sometimes a website fights back, and big jobs take a few minutes. But it tells you straight when something's blocked, and it tries another way before giving up.

## Verdict

Chatbots gave us answers. Hark gives you time back. This is the next generation of AI use: not a smarter search box, but something that actually takes things off your plate. 10/10, and again, this isn't sponsored. I'm just not going back.
