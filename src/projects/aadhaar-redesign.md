## The Problem

The Aadhaar portal is something every Indian needs, but it's hard to use for elderly citizens, rural and low-literacy users, people with visual impairments and anyone who doesn't read English comfortably.

## What I Built

- **Language first:** the prototype opens on a language picker, in English, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali and Marathi.
- **Accessibility:** adjustable text size, a high-contrast mode, keyboard navigation, 44px touch targets and alt text throughout, aiming for WCAG 2.1 AA.
- **Voice:** hover-to-speak text-to-speech, so people can hear the interface instead of reading it.
- **Services:** step-by-step mock flows for downloading Aadhaar, updating details, booking an appointment, verifying a number, generating a Virtual ID and locking biometrics, with progress saved in local storage.
