## The Problem

Classic property games drag: one runaway leader, long dead turns and trades nobody trusts. I wanted to keep the buy-the-world fantasy and fix the parts that make people quit halfway.

## What I Built

- **Game:** a full board game in Godot (GDScript) for 2 to 6 players, played online, on a local network or hot-seat on one PC, with CPU opponents to fill seats.
- **Trading that means something:** auctions on by default, and trade promises like rent-free visits, IOUs and rent shares.
- **Twists:** ability cards, Chaos Mode, secret missions, 2v2 teams and underdog rules that keep losing players in the game.
- **Progression:** levels and XP, seasons, a daily challenge, achievements and replays.
- **Website:** a plain HTML/CSS/JS marketing site with a press kit, deployed on Vercel.

## Status

In active development and aimed at a Steam release.
