## The Idea

Roll. Buy. Build. Repeat. Pixopoly keeps the buy-the-world fantasy of classic property games and fixes the parts that make people quit halfway: one runaway leader, long dead turns and trades nobody trusts.

## Features

- **Real cities, drawn in pixels:** Paris, Tokyo, Rio, Istanbul, Cairo, Toronto, Mumbai and more.
- **Play with friends:** 2 to 6 players online, on a local network or on one couch, with CPU opponents to fill seats.
- **Dice, deals and a little betrayal:** auctions, and trades that can carry promises like rent-free visits and IOUs.
- **Twists:** ability cards, Chaos Mode, secret missions, 2v2 teams and underdog rules that keep everyone in the game.

## Status

In development and coming soon to Steam.
