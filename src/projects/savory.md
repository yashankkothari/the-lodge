## The Problem

X is where you find great ideas, and its bookmarks are where they disappear: one endless list with no search, no folders and no way to remember why you saved something.

## What It Does

- **Save with context:** hit bookmark on any post and a small panel appears right on x.com to add tags, pick a folder and jot a note.
- **Search by meaning:** find the post you're thinking of even when it uses different words, plus instant full-text search across posts, links, authors, tags and notes.
- **Folders, tags and notes:** colour-coded folders, automatic topic tags, and a searchable note on every bookmark.
- **Media gallery:** every saved image, video and GIF on one wall, with a full-screen viewer, a built-in video player and original-quality downloads.
- **Live sync:** imports your whole bookmark history on its own, then picks up new bookmarks within seconds, including ones saved on your phone.
- **Sort and filter:** by bookmark date, post date, likes, reposts, views, author, type and more, all from the keyboard.
- **Multiple accounts:** a separate library for each X account.
- **Private by design:** no account, no servers, no tracking. Everything stays in your browser, and you can export it any time.

## Status

Live and free on the Chrome Web Store, working in Chrome, Edge, Brave and Arc. A Pro plan with AI summaries, Q&A over your bookmarks and sync is on the way.
